#### Writing Email

```
You are writing to a friend or close work/study mate. Write about 60 words.

1. Change of plan. We agreed to… but…
    
2. Have you heard …?
    
3. You won’t believe the new place that just opened near my house…
    
4. A few of us are planning to …
    

  

- Send your email to all the other people in your group (do a ‘reply to all’). 
    
- Respond to the emails you receive – always do a ‘reply to all’ so that your teacher is included in your mail exchanges.
```
